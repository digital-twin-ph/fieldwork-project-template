THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL IN THIS PROJECT
====================================

Replace this file's contents with the terms that apply to what this project carries,
and delete the guidance below once you have.

Record one section per source. A section should say, at minimum:

  Source:   who published it, and where it was obtained
  Licence:  the licence, by name and link
  What was taken, and what was done to it

Two things are worth stating that are easy to leave out:

SHARE-ALIKE REACHES THIS REPOSITORY. Data under a licence such as the ODbL makes a
work derived from it subject to the same terms. If a project file carries that data --
not merely a figure drawn from it -- then this repository is a Derivative Database in
part, and the obligation attaches here and not only to what you publish.

ATTRIBUTION IS A CONDITION OF USE, NOT A COURTESY. A figure leaves the application and
travels away from whatever page credited its sources. If the licence requires credit,
the credit belongs on the figure.

WHAT IS NOT COMMITTED STILL HAS TERMS. A payload reproduced from a declaration rather
than committed -- see assets/README.md -- is still governed by the licence of the
source it is read from, and that licence should be recorded here even though the bytes
are not in this repository.
